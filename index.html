★ FEATURED · MARKETS

Crude Oil Signal Engine

A Python system that scores crude oil swing-trade setups every weekday. Four signal layers (trend, physical supply, macro and cross-asset) combine into one score, which drives volatility-targeted position sizing with ATR-based stops.

It ships with a backtester and runs on a schedule in GitHub Actions, emailing a daily report before the Sydney open. I use it to paper-trade my own oil positions before putting real money behind it.

★ FEATURED · EMISSIONS & COMPLIANCE

ASX Energy Emissions Tracker

Which ASX-listed energy producers are really cutting emissions, and which are meeting their targets by buying offsets? This pipeline works through the Clean Energy Regulator's Safeguard Mechanism and NGER data to separate facility-level reductions from reliance on ACCU surrenders, then checks the results against company disclosures and transition plans.

about

I'm a final-year Data Science and Decisions student at UNSW Sydney, heading into energy and resources.

I came to energy through the market itself. I trade crude oil on my own account, and I wanted a more rigorous way to read it than chart patterns. That turned into the signal engine above, and from there into what Australia's biggest emitters actually report to the regulator.

I grew up around trade. My family runs Rapid Cargo, a freight-forwarding and customs business in Pakistan, so shipping costs, tariffs and commodity prices were everyday conversation long before they were datasets.

Away from the screen I work client-facing at M.J. Bale, where I've learnt to explain things to people who don't care about the method.
